Problem Analysis
Critical Flaws in Conventional Ticketing

Ticket Scalping & Fake Passes

Static QR codes and screenshot sharing allow bad actors to replicate passes, resell duplicate barcodes, and create gate chaos.

Blind Seat Selection

Standard drop-down menus hide venue geometry. Buyers have no sense of angle, stage distance, acoustic clarity, or obstructed sightlines.

Multi-Track Decision Fatigue

Large summits overwhelm attendees with concurrent conflicting keynotes and leave relevant peer networking to pure chance.

Disconnected Group Reservations

Hackathon teams and corporate groups cannot easily coordinate seat blocks, share invite codes, or receive group volume discounts.
